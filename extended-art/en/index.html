AI EXTENDED ART

Extend the scenery of your Pokémon cards

FUNY PIN Extended Art expands the illustration beyond a card’s artwork window and creates a printable design to display around your physical card. Choose a supported card from the Pokémon 30th anniversary pack and create your own display.

Create extended art ›

Sign in to search for cards and generate artwork.

How to create extended art

  1. Search for a card

    Search by card name or number. Selected Korean, Japanese and English cards with a regular artwork window are supported. Check availability in the search results.

  2. Check the cutout and alignment

    Confirm the artwork cutout and generate AI extended art. Check the alignment between the original and extended image, then use the preview controls for text and symbols, the 30th anniversary symbol, borders and corners.

  3. Download the printable PDF

    Download your finished design as a PDF and print at actual size, 100% scale. Cut the printed outline and artwork window, then place the design in front of the protective sleeve for display.

Print size and display instructions

The finished design measures 64 × 89mm. Turn off “Fit to page” in your print settings and check the scale using the 10mm reference line. Keep the original card intact and inside a protective sleeve.

Frequently asked questions

Are Chinese-language cards supported?

The current card catalog supports Korean, Japanese and English cards. Guide languages and supported card languages are provided separately.

Which interface languages are available?

Use the 한 / EN buttons in the editor header to switch between Korean and English.

Can I use any card?

Only supported cards from the 30th anniversary pack can be used. Cards without a regular artwork window may be unavailable. Check the search results for availability.
